# Civic Issue Reporting Problem Statement

Municipal civic reporting is fundamentally broken across four systemic vectors:

1. **Fragmented Reporting**: Citizens navigate disconnected portals, telephone hotlines, and social media handles with inconsistent submission requirements and zero workflow coordination.
2. **Department Ambiguity**: Citizens cannot reliably determine whether a hazard belongs to Solid Waste, Public Works, Storm Water, or Electrical utilities, causing complaints to bounce between departments for weeks.
3. **Duplicate Complaint Floods**: High-visibility hazards generate dozens of independent citizen reports at the same location, overwhelming municipal desks and scattering citizen communication across disconnected tickets.
4. **Black-Hole Resolution Visibility**: Reporters receive static tracking numbers with no estimated resolution SLA, actionable timeline milestones, or transparent progress tracking.

CivicMind resolves these breakdowns through an autonomous multi-agent pipeline that ingests raw images and descriptions, classifies hazards, merges duplicates into canonical issues, routes responsible departments, and delivers proactive notifications.
